    Insight

    Insight is the Institute’s essay series of reflections and proposals on humans and the future.
    Contributors include the Institute’s researchers, scholars from a wide range of related disciplines, activists and practitioners in the field, and educators.
    Issues are published in Korean and also made available in English.

    English editions are listed here as they become available.
