    Welcome

    The Institute for Human & Future is an interdisciplinary research institute
    based at Chuncheon National University of Education, Korea.

    Leading future discourse centered on human values
    in an era of technological transformation

    Today’s technological change asks not only what humans can do, but what it means to be human. Bringing together researchers in science education, the humanities, the social sciences, education, and technology, the Institute studies how technology, the humanities and society, and the environment are changing together — and shares its findings with schools, local communities, and civil society.
