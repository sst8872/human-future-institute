    Greeting

    Shaping a human-centered discourse on the future

    Technology has continually expanded the possibilities of human life. Yet today’s technological transformation goes beyond the question of what humans can do and asks us anew what it is to be human. The Institute for Humanity & Future was founded to take up this question as a subject of scholarly inquiry.

    Why the humanities and social sciences matter more than ever

    The future is something we choose before it is something we predict. Deciding what kind of future is desirable cannot rest on technological efficiency alone; it calls for reflection on human dignity, justice in our communities, and coexistence with nature. This is why the humanities and social sciences are needed all the more in an age of technology.

    An interdisciplinary research community

    The Institute aims to be an interdisciplinary community in which researchers from science education, the humanities, the social sciences, education, and science and technology work together. Where different disciplinary perspectives meet, we seek a balanced understanding of the future and alternatives that can be put into practice.

    To schools and to society

    Building on rigorous research and open scholarly exchange, the Institute will return its findings to classrooms and to society, and contribute to shaping a human-centered discourse on the future.

    Thank you.

    Director, Institute for Humanity & Future Heojeong Yoon
