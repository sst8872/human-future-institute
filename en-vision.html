    Vision

    Vision

    Leading human-centered discourse on the future
    in an era of technological transformation

    Purposes

    1. 01Exploring enduring human values

      In an age when technology is reshaping human capabilities and boundaries, we explore the values that make us human, such as dignity and autonomy, relationships and empathy.

    2. 02Setting directions for education and society

      Reading changes in technology, humanity and society, and the environment together, we propose concrete directions for education and the tasks society must prepare for.

    3. 03Renewing and sharing the value of the humanities and social sciences

      In debates about the future that center on technology, we reaffirm the academic and social significance of the humanities and social sciences, and share our research with schools, local communities, and civil society.
